I had sex with a tomato

04/11/2026


Okay I know what you're thinking. This guy had sex with a tomato? and no. I didn't.

When I was 13, I was on the school bus. Right? I was known for being a bit unhinged. And so during a conversation, I don't remember why, but I said "I had sex with a tomato". The bus driver heard. She's a nice lady, and she didn't hear correctly, so we had a discussion (I'm at the back of the bus. It's a bit awkward) heres how it kind of went

"Are you eating on the bus?"

"What??? No"

"Mmm... (my name), you cannot be eating"

"Yes, I know"

"What were you talking about?"

well shit. she's onto me.

"Just my favorite foods."

"Tomatoes?"

"Yes. I love tomatoes."

Thinking back on it, she must've thought I was mentally unstable and needed a doctor. Who's favorite food is a fucking tomato? I didn't see the concern in her eyes but i bet she went home and told her signifigant other "I met this kid who likes tomatoes". Think of the suprise on this hypothetical persons face.

My brother also heard me say it, in 100 percent clarity. He's younger, and he absolutely razzed me on it. My parents aren't very "ohh, yeah you can say something along the lines of 'I had sex with a tomato' and get away with it". He made fun of me, and maybe blackmailed me a bit. I felt hurt and betrayed. But i guess its fine, because I also blackmailed him hundreds of times before.

Even now, looking back on it, I'm confused. What the fuck was going through my puny little mind? I blame our education systems. They should've told us to never claim you had sex with vegetables (SHIT. TOMATOES ARE FRUITS, ARENT THEY.) Think of the lives they could've saved... instead, I got a smoking and vaping unit, safe sex lessons, and meditation skills. To this day, the number of kids who claimed they fucked a plant will never get their childhoods back. Amen.

My lesson to you? Don't tell people you had sex with a tomato. It really makes a difference if you take a stand, and don't tell people you had sex with a tomato.



Okay, yeah, I'm done here.
